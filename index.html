Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026 · Last updated: 11 September 2026

The short version

Doughmatics doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no tracking, and no ads. Your recipes stay on your device — we never see them.

01Who we are

Doughmatics (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a baker's-percentage calculator for pizza, sourdough and bread doughs. This policy explains how the app handles information. The app is provided by Craig Edward Davey.

02Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Doughmatics has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, location, or any other personal detail.

The app does not contain analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking software of any kind, and makes no network requests of its own.

03Information stored on your device

Everything you create in Doughmatics is stored locally on your device only:

  • Recipes — every dough you save, including quantities, hydration, salt, fat, sugar, fermentation schedule and any notes you add.
  • Favourites — which saved recipes you've starred.
  • App preferences — settings such as your chosen unit (grams or ounces), appearance (light, dark or auto), default dough type, and mixing-loss allowance.

This information never leaves your device on its own, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and is removed when you delete the app.

04iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on iCloud Backup in Settings, your recipes are mirrored to your own private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit, so they can sync across your own devices. This is off by default, controlled entirely by you, and that data is never accessible to us — it stays inside your personal Apple account, the same as any other iCloud data.

05How information is used

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use it for any purpose. Information stored on your device is used solely to provide the app's features — calculating and displaying your recipes and remembering your preferences.

06Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any information, because we do not collect any. No data is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

07Children's privacy

Doughmatics does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly gather data from children under 13.

08Your rights and choices

Since no personal data is collected or stored off your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control: turn iCloud Backup off at any time in Settings, or delete the app to remove all locally stored information.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if the app adds new features. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be reflected in an app update.

10Support & contact

This page also serves as Doughmatics' support contact. If you have a question about how the app works, run into a bug, or have any question about this Privacy Policy, reach us at craig-davey96@outlook.com.
